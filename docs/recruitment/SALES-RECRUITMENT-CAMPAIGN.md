## Application structure

The application takes approximately 15–20 minutes and has three parts:

1. Basics and employment readiness: contact information, Phoenix availability, work authorization, travel readiness, start date, sales background, resume, and optional supporting documents.
2. Gardening focus: practical growing experience, plants and environments, organic practices, product familiarity, and a soil-biology customer question.
3. Computer literacy and sales skills: digital-tool experience, software familiarity, customer follow-up workflow, and trust-building example.

Applicants may add up to five optional supporting files, including a cover letter, references, certificates, or work samples. Do not request identification, Social Security information, banking details, or medical records during this application stage.

## Best-fit candidate

The strongest candidate combines sales or customer-service ability with real interest in gardening, organic growing, soil biology, landscaping, nurseries, farming, or Soil Seed & Water products.

## Job-board description

### Sales Representative — Phoenix, Arizona

Soil Seed & Water is seeking a Sales Representative who can help gardeners and growers understand their soil, choose the right products, and get better results.

Responsibilities include responding to customer needs, following up with leads, explaining product options, building long-term relationships, and keeping sales activity organized.

Preferred experience includes home gardening, professional growing, landscaping, nursery or garden-center work, organic growing, soil biology, regenerative agriculture, or use of Soil Seed & Water products. Sales or customer-service experience and clear communication are important.

This is a Phoenix-area role. Schedule, employment structure, and compensation will be discussed with qualified applicants during the hiring process.

Apply at https://www.organicsoilwholesale.com/careers/sales

## Applicant review scorecard

Score each area from 0 to 3:

- Communication: clear, direct, helpful answers.
- Sales ability: trust-building, follow-up, and problem-solving examples.
- Growing knowledge: practical gardening, soil, landscape, nursery, or farm experience.
- Product connection: has used or can quickly learn Soil Seed & Water products.
- Computer readiness: can use or quickly learn email, calendars, spreadsheets, CRM/order tools, and follow-up workflows.
- Local fit: able to work in the Phoenix area and start within the needed timeframe.

Use the score to organize review, not as an automatic rejection rule.

## Phone screen

1. Tell us about what you grow or your experience working with plants and soil.
2. What kind of sales environment brings out your best work?
3. How do you follow up when a customer is interested but not ready to buy?
4. Explain compost, fertilizer, and soil biology to a new gardener.
5. What would you want to learn first about our products?
6. What schedule and compensation structure are you seeking?
7. If selected, when could you start?
