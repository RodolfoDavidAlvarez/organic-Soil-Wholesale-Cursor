# Signup and follow-up

## Page headline

**Win a complete fall garden—delivered to your door.**

Two raised beds. Soil. Compost. Worm castings. Mulch. One Phoenix-area winner.

CTA: **Enter the giveaway**

## Form

Required:

- First and last name
- Email
- Mobile phone
- ZIP code
- New garden or existing garden
- Project type: home, school, community garden, landscape client, other
- Number of beds and approximate dimensions
- What they grow: vegetables, herbs, roses, citrus, stone fruit, grapes, avocado, palms, ornamentals, natives/succulents, turf, other
- Preferred buying size: a few bags, bundle, pallet, tote, bulk delivery
- “If you win, do you have space and access for pallet delivery?” yes/no/not sure
- Acceptance of Official Rules

Optional and separate:

- Short project description
- Marketing email/SMS consent. Entry cannot depend on this consent.

Do not collect the full delivery address until someone is a finalist or winner.

## Confirmation page

“You’re entered. We’ll email your entry confirmation. The winner will be selected after the giveaway closes.”

Then show the three current garden offers without requiring a purchase:

- Existing bed → Garden Refresh, $99
- One bed → Garden Refresh Plus, $149
- Two to three beds → Big Garden Setup, $399

## Follow-up

- Immediately: entry confirmation + share link
- Day 2: show what is inside the prize
- Day 5: garden education email based on what they grow
- Final 72 hours: deadline reminders
- Winner: direct verification, acceptance, delivery scheduling
- Non-winners: 72-hour garden offer matched to their answers

No extra entry or better odds for purchasing. If referrals or social sharing are used, legal review is required before promising extra entries.

## Tracking

- Valid entries and duplicate rate
- Cost per valid entry
- Form completion rate
- Profile completion by plant/project type
- Email and SMS opt-in rates, separately
- Winner content captured
- Non-winner offer conversion
- Revenue and gross margin by offer
- Repeat purchase within 30/60/90 days

## Official-rules checklist

Before launch, publish reviewed rules containing sponsor, start/end date and time zone, eligibility, geographic area, age, no-purchase statement, entry limit, prize description, exact ARV, delivery limits, winner-selection method, odds statement, notification deadline, alternate-winner process, taxes, privacy, publicity/release terms, and void-where-prohibited language.
