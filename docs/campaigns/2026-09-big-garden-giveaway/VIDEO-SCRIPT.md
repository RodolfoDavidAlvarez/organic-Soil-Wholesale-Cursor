# Video shoot — Big Garden Giveaway

Goal: use a 30-minute recording session to produce one 45–60 second main video plus short clips.

## Main script

**Hook — at the garden beds**

“Phoenix, we gave away worm castings in August. In September, we’re going much bigger.”

**Reveal — walk past the full bundle**

“One local gardener is getting an entire fall garden delivered to their door.”

“That means two raised garden beds, four pallets of PlantPal growing mix, a mixed pallet with Simon’s Gold dairy compost and Mikey’s Worm Poop, plus a full pallet of Nature’s Blanket mulch.”

**Why**

“We want one family, school, or community garden to have what it needs to start growing this fall.”

**CTA**

“Entering is free. Tap the link, tell us what you’re growing, and enter the Big Garden Giveaway. One winner. No purchase necessary.”

**Close**

“We’re Soil Seed & Water in Phoenix. Let’s grow something.”

## 30-minute shoot plan

- 0:00–3:00 — establish the yard, beds, pallets, and delivery truck
- 3:00–8:00 — record the hook five ways
- 8:00–15:00 — record the complete script three times
- 15:00–22:00 — close-ups: PlantPal, compost, worm castings, mulch, steel posts, wood
- 22:00–26:00 — demonstrate how the bed corners and wood assemble
- 26:00–30:00 — record CTA and deadline clips with clean audio

## Short hooks

- “What if your entire fall garden showed up at your door?”
- “We’re giving one Phoenix gardener the soil, beds, compost, castings, and mulch to start growing.”
- “August was one bag. September is the whole garden.”

## Shot checklist

- All six product/material groups together
- Two real bed kits, not a generic bed photo
- A hand holding each soil texture
- Forklift or pallet movement
- Delivery vehicle
- Rodo speaking directly to camera
- Clean vertical and horizontal takes

Do not say “$5,000 value” on camera until Mike confirms the bed-kit and delivery values.
