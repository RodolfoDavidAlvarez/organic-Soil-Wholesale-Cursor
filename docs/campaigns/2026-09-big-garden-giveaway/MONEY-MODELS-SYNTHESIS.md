# $100M Money Models — SSW working synthesis

Alex Hormozi's core idea is to arrange offers so the cash from a new customer helps pay to acquire and serve more customers quickly. The four working stages are attraction, upsell, downsell, and continuity.

## Complete model list recovered from the book/course notes

Attraction:

- Win Your Money Back
- Giveaways
- Decoy
- Buy X Get Y Free
- Pay Less Now or Pay More Later
- Free Goodwill

Upsell:

- Classic Upsell
- Menu Upsell
- Anchor Upsell
- Rollover Upsell

Downsell:

- Payment Plan
- Trial With Penalty
- Feature Downsell

Continuity:

- Bonus
- Discount
- Waived Fee

## SSW application

- Attraction: September Big Garden Giveaway
- Qualification: ask project, new/existing garden, what they grow, timing, and buying format
- Classic/menu upsell: recommend the soil prescription and one relevant amendment
- Anchor: show the complete setup before smaller packages
- Feature downsell: Garden Refresh Plus or Garden Refresh
- Rollover: allow a starter purchase or benefit to roll into a larger package where the economics are approved
- Continuity: seasonal garden replenishment by crop and Phoenix planting cycle
- Reactivation: use the same customer profile for future citrus, roses, grape, turf, palm, and vegetable campaigns

## September sequence

1. Free giveaway entry.
2. Collect useful garden data.
3. One winner receives the complete setup.
4. Non-winners receive the best matching $99, $149, or $399 offer for 72 hours.
5. Sales contacts only the highest-intent leads.
6. Buyers receive education, an appropriate add-on, and a future replenishment recommendation.

## Guardrails

- Do not make purchase, referral, class attendance, testimonial, or marketing consent a condition of entry without legal review.
- Keep the giveaway and the runner-up sale clearly separate.
- Use one simple hero offer. Do not place all Money Models on one customer-facing page.
- Measure gross margin and delivery cost, not revenue alone.
