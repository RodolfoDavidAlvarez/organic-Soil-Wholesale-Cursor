# Organic Soil Wholesale Purchase Flow

## Overview

The simplified purchase flow enables customers to easily select products with appropriate packaging options, specify quantity, and complete orders efficiently.

## Purchase Flow Steps

### Step 1: Product Selection

- User selects product category (Soil Amendments, Potting Soils, etc.)
- User chooses specific product from the category
- User selects packaging option (size category):
  - Pallet of Boxes (144 units/36 boxes)
  - Pallet of Bags (50 bags, 1CF)
  - 2.2 CY Tote (supersack)
  - Bulk Delivery (22-24 tons per truckload)
  - Bulk Pickup (cubic yards, Vicksburg location only)
- User enters quantity
- Multiple products can be added to the order

### Step 2: Contact Information

- Business Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number

### Step 3: Delivery Options

- User selects delivery or pickup:
  - **Delivery**: Enter delivery address (required for bulk delivery)
  - **Pickup**: Select location (Phoenix, Parker, or Vicksburg)
    - Vicksburg limited to Dairy Compost and Worm Castings only

### Step 4: Order Review

- Review products, quantities, and sizing
- Review contact and delivery information
- View estimated lead times
- Submit order

### Step 5: Order Confirmation

- Thank you message displayed
- Order confirmation sent to customer and admin

## Product Constraints

### Size Categories

| Size Category | Units | Truckload Discount | Mixing Allowed |
|---------------|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| Pallet of Boxes | 144 units per pallet | Yes (22+ pallets) | Within category |
| Pallet of Bags | 50 bags per pallet | Yes (22+ pallets) | Within category |
| 2.2 CY Tote | 1 tote per pallet | Yes (22+ pallets) | Within category |
| Bulk Delivery | 22-24 tons per truckload | Full truckload only | No mixing |
| Bulk Pickup | No minimum | Not eligible | N/A |

### Location Restrictions

- **Phoenix & Parker**: All products and size categories available
- **Vicksburg**: Only Dairy Compost and Worm Castings in bulk format

### Discount Rules

- 20% discount on full truckloads (22 pallets or equivalent)
- Discount applies only to complete truckloads

## Validation Rules

1. Bulk orders require delivery (cannot be picked up)
2. Only specific products allowed for Bulk Pickup (Dairy Compost, Worm Castings)
3. Vicksburg pickup location limited to specific products
4. Cannot mix products between different size categories
