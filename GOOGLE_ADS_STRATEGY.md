# Organic Soil Wholesale - Google Ads & SEO Strategy Guide

## Website Overview

Organic Soil Wholesale is an ecommerce platform focused exclusively on **bulk soil products** for **commercial buyers**. The primary target audiences are:

- Professional landscapers
- Commercial growers
- Farms and nurseries
- Golf courses
- Vineyard operators

The website sells premium organic soil products in wholesale quantities only:

- Pallets of bags
- Pallets of boxes
- Supersacks (2.2 CY totes)
- Bulk delivery (truckloads)
- Bulk pickup by cubic yard

The business primarily serves Arizona with delivery options available to commercial addresses.

## Product Categories

1. **Soil Amendments** (highest priority)

   - Dairy Compost (flagship product)
   - Worm Castings (flagship product)
   - Biochar Mineral
   - Zeolite

2. **Specialized Blends**

   - Turf Daddy Blend (for landscapers/golf courses)
   - Artemis Root Boost (for tree/shrub planting)
   - Bacchus Blend (for vineyards)
   - Desert Defender (for drought conditions)

3. **Potting Soils**

   - Ready Go Garden
   - CannaBag (for cannabis growers)

4. **Mulch Products**
   - Various mulch types for landscapers

## SEO Implementation Details

### 1. Meta Tags & Technical SEO

- Implemented comprehensive metadata targeting wholesale/commercial buyers
- Added structured data (schema.org) for business, products, and services
- Created robots.txt and sitemap.xml for proper indexing
- Added canonical URLs to prevent duplicate content issues
- Implemented dynamic page-specific meta tags (title, description, keywords)
- Used geo-targeting tags for Arizona focus

## Google Ads Strategy Recommendations

### 1. Campaign Structure

Recommend organizing campaigns by buyer persona:

1. **Landscapers Campaign**

   - Focus on mulch, turf blends, soil amendments
   - Target keywords related to commercial landscaping

2. **Growers Campaign**

   - Focus on amendments, potting soils, specialized blends
   - Target keywords related to commercial growing operations

3. **Golf Course Campaign**

   - Focus on specialized products for turf management
   - Narrow targeting to golf course maintenance professionals

4. **Generic Wholesale Campaign**
   - Focus on bulk delivery and general wholesale terms
   - Target broader commercial/wholesale keywords

### 2. Ad Extensions to Utilize

- **Callout Extensions**: "Truckload Delivery", "Pallet Quantities Only", "Commercial Accounts", "Wholesale Pricing"
- **Structured Snippets**: Highlight product categories
- **Price Extensions**: Show bulk pricing tiers
- **Location Extensions**: Show Arizona service area
- **Call Extensions**: Direct line for commercial inquiries

### 3. Landing Page Strategy

| Campaign          | Primary Landing Page         | Secondary Landing Pages                          |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Landscapers       | /landscapers                 | /products?category=mulch                         |
| Growers           | /products?category=amendment | /products/dairy-compost, /products/worm-castings |
| Golf Course       | /products/turf-daddy-blend   | /landscapers                                     |
| Generic Wholesale | / (homepage)                 | /order                                           |

### 4. Audience Targeting

- **Custom Intent Audiences**: People searching for bulk soil, landscaping supplies, commercial growing supplies
- **Custom Affinity Audiences**: Professional landscapers, nursery owners, farm operators
- **B2B Targeting**: Company size, industry vertical (agriculture, landscaping)
- **Geo-targeting**: Arizona primarily, with emphasis on major commercial/agricultural areas

### 5. Tracking & Conversion Setup

- Implement conversion tracking for all form submissions
- Set up separate conversion values for different form types
- Track phone calls from ads
- Consider implementing enhanced ecommerce tracking
- Set up remarketing tags to capture interested wholesale buyers

## Unique Value Propositions to Emphasize

1. **Commercial-Only Focus**: Specialized in wholesale quantities, not a retail business
2. **Premium Organic Products**: OMRI and US Compost Council certified
3. **Specialized Blends**: Custom formulations for specific commercial applications
4. **Arizona Experts**: Products optimized for local growing conditions
5. **Multiple Delivery Options**: Flexible options for commercial clients
