### Admin Form Tips
- The **Available Sizes** field in the new admin form expects a comma-separated list—paste the values exactly as shown above.
- Texture photos should load first on Pay & Pickup, so double-check those URLs before saving.
- Price is per smallest unit (9lb bag or 2 cu ft bag). Adjust the inventory count after bulk deliveries using Admin ➜ Inventory.
