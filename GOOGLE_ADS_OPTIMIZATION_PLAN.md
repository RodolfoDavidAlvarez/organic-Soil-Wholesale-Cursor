# Google Ads Optimization Plan - Landscapers Page

## Background
After one week of Google Ads data collection, we have identified key search patterns and user behavior that will guide our optimization strategy.

## Key Findings from Google Ads Data
- **Top Search Term**: "Bulk Soil" - This is what users are searching for most
- **Target Page**: Landscapers page - Primary landing page for optimization
- **Missing Elements**: Pricing information and clearer product positioning

## Optimization Strategy

### Phase 1: Product Positioning
1. **Primary Focus**: Position organic soil as multi-purpose, best-in-class solution
2. **Messaging**: Emphasize "bulk soil" terminology to match search intent
3. **Value Proposition**: Highlight organic certification and versatility

### Phase 2: Pricing Integration
1. Add transparent pricing information to landscapers page
2. Display bulk pricing tiers
3. Include cost-per-yard or cost-per-ton breakdowns
4. Show volume discounts clearly

### Phase 3: Landing Page Optimization
1. Update hero section to emphasize "bulk organic soil"
2. Restructure content hierarchy to lead with soil products
3. Add pricing tables and calculators
4. Improve call-to-action buttons

## Implementation Notes
- Start with landscapers page modifications
- Focus on bulk soil messaging alignment
- Ensure pricing transparency
- Maintain organic certification emphasis

## Success Metrics
- Improved ad relevance scores
- Higher conversion rates from ads
- Increased time on page
- More quote requests and orders
